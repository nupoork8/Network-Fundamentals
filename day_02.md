## 1. What Is the Internet?

The internet is a worldwide collection of interconnected
networks that communicate using common protocols and standards.

These networks exchange information through different
transmission methods.

### Transmission media

- Copper cables
- Fiber-optic cables
- Wireless transmission
- Satellite links

## 2. Types of Networks

### Home Network
A small network connecting devices such as computers and
phones to each other and to the internet.

### SOHO Network
SOHO stands for Small Office/Home Office. These networks
support devices used in small offices and home offices.

### Medium-to-Large Networks
These networks can connect many devices across offices,
schools, organizations, or multiple locations.

### The Internet
The internet connects networks around the world.

## 3. How Is Data Transmitted?

Digital data is represented as bits (0s and 1s).

For data to travel between devices, it is encoded and
transmitted using signals through a communication medium.

### Electrical Transmission
- Uses electrical signals.
- Commonly used with copper cables.

### Optical Transmission
- Uses light signals.
- Used in fiber-optic cables.

### Wireless Transmission
- Uses electromagnetic waves.
- Examples include radio and microwave communication.

## 4. Categories of Data

### Volunteered Data
Information that people intentionally provide.

Example: Entering information into an online form.

### Inferred Data
Information derived by analyzing other data.

Example: A credit score calculated using relevant financial
information.

### Observed Data
Information collected by recording people's actions or
interactions.

Example: Location data collected by a mobile phone, subject
to device settings and applicable permissions.

## 5. Key Takeaways

- The internet is a network of interconnected networks.
- Networks can range from small home networks to global networks.
- Data is represented digitally as bits.
- Signals carry information through different transmission media.
- Copper, fiber-optic, and wireless technologies transmit data
  in different ways.
- Volunteered, inferred, and observed data describe different
  ways information is provided, derived, or collected.
